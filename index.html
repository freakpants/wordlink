How to Play

  1. Each day you get two anchor words (shown in blue and red) placed on the canvas.
  2. Click the canvas to pick where your next word will appear.
  3. Type a word and press Enter or click Add.
  4. Two words connect when they are physically close and semantically similar.
  5. Build a chain of connected words from the blue word to the red word.
  6. Your score = number of bridge words used. Fewer is better!

💡 Drag any bridge word to reposition it and form new connections.
