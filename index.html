How to Play

  1. Each day you get two anchor words (shown in blue and red) placed on the canvas.
  2. Type a bridge word and WordLink will place it automatically.
  3. Press Enter or click Add to submit it.
  4. Gray bubbles are your bridge words. Links appear at 16.00%+ similarity — these are the connections that count.
  5. Click any bubble to see how close it is to every other word on a clearer 0-100% scale with two decimals.
  6. Build a chain of connected words from the blue word to the red word.
  7. Your score = number of bridge words used. Fewer is better!

Drag any bridge word to reposition it and form new connections. Practice links use a bare puzzle number in the URL for easy sharing.
